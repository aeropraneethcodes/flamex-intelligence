# Online Guided Tour

The **Guided Tour** is the second navigation entry, directly after Mission overview. It is intended for independent reviewers and judges.

1. Open **Mission overview** and choose a scientific spotlight.
2. Open **Guided Tour** and try the five evidence-audited questions.
3. Use **Combustion explorer** and **Fuel comparison lab** to examine actual FLEX records and regressions.
4. Ask **FlameIQ**: “What do the fuel regressions reveal and what can they not prove?”
5. Visit **NASA data lab** to distinguish bundled raw FLEX records from other NASA investigation metadata.

The evidence-only assistant does not require an API key. No raw BASS-II, Saffire, or SAME measurements are bundled. The app is a research prototype and does not certify spacecraft fire safety.
